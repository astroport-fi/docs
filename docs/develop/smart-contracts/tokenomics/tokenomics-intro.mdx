# Tokenomics

Under long-term support there are no ASTRO emissions and no governance token. Protocol fees are collected by the Maker on each chain: Neutron fees buy and burn ASTRO, and Terra fees are split between burning ASTRO, the Terra Development Fund and yASTRO stakers.

- [**Maker**](./maker): collects pool fees, swaps them into a base asset and splits it across legs.
- [**ASTRO Burner**](./astro-burner): the ASTRO denom admin on Neutron, which can only burn.
- [**yASTRO**](./yastro): stake ASTRO on Terra for yASTRO and earn a share of Terra fees.
- [**Incentives**](./incentives): stake LP tokens and earn external incentive schedules.
- [**Vesting**](./vesting): the schedules that funded ASTRO emissions, all ended.
- [**xASTRO Staking (retired)**](./staking): what happened to xASTRO and the payout to holders.
