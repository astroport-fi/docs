# xASTRO Staking (retired)

<Admonition type="warning" title="Retired">
  xASTRO staking is retired. xASTRO has no value and can't be redeemed. To stake ASTRO today, use [yASTRO](./yastro) on Terra.
</Admonition>

## Overview

The xASTRO staking contract let ASTRO holders stake ASTRO for xASTRO. The ASTRO behind each xASTRO grew as the Maker sent part of the protocol's fees to the staking contract, and `leave` burned xASTRO for its share of the staked ASTRO. xASTRO was also the token used for Astroport governance, which is retired.

On 2026-09-22 a Neutron governance proposal (Neutron prop 9) took control of eight Astroport contracts on Neutron and drained 370.3M ASTRO from xASTRO staking and six pools. Neutron halted and restarted on 2026-09-25, and the funds were returned to a recovery multisig.

## What happened to the contract

The Neutron staking contract was frozen and then migrated to `astroport-staking` 2.3.3:

- Staking is paused: `enter`, `enter_with_hook` and `leave` all fail with `Paused`.
- The 2.3.3 migration's `withdraw_astro` option moved the contract's ASTRO out. After a withdrawal the contract is marked retired and stays paused for good: a later migration can't set another mode.
- Its queries still answer, so past balances can be looked up.

The Maker no longer sends anything to xASTRO staking. Under long-term support, Neutron fees are burned and a quarter of Terra fees goes to [yASTRO](./yastro) stakers. See [Maker](./maker).

## Payout to xASTRO holders

Every pre-incident xASTRO holder was paid in ASTRO at **1.3402 ASTRO per xASTRO** (exactly 370,318,691.388430 ASTRO / 276,322,268.374898 xASTRO, the rate at Neutron block 61,633,114, before the incident).

- On Neutron, 1,788 wallets were paid 327.3M ASTRO, including refunds of 4.99M ASTRO to wallets that deposited after the incident.
- On Terra, 57 wallets were paid 47.9M ASTRO, from balances at Terra block 22,948,000.
- xASTRO held in contracts (vaults, LP positions, ve3, Eclipse) was looked through and paid to the underlying wallets. DAOs were paid at their own addresses.

The payments were made with the `astroport-xastro-payout` 1.0.0 contracts. Each stores the Merkle root of a fixed `(address, amount)` list, pays each address at most once, and always pays the address in the leaf. Both payouts are finished.

## The older Terra CW20 staking

Before ASTRO moved to Neutron, xASTRO staking ran on Terra as a CW20 contract (`astroport-staking` 1.0.0) with a CW20 xASTRO token (`astroport-xastro-token` 1.0.0). Both are legacy contracts and not part of the protocol.
