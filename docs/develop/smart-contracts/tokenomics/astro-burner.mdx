# ASTRO Burner

## Overview

The burner removes a tokenfactory denom from supply. It burns its whole balance of the denom with tokenfactory `MsgBurn`, so the burner must be the denom's admin.

Anyone can trigger a burn. Funds can reach the burner in two ways:

- They are attached to `burn`. The [Maker](./maker) does this with a `deposit` leg whose `msg` is `{"burn":{}}`.
- They are sent to the burner directly, with a later `burn` call from anyone.

The burner has no message that mints or that changes the denom's admin.

## How it's used

On Neutron, the burner is the admin of the ASTRO denom `factory/neutron1ffus553eet978k024lmssw0czsxwr97mggyv85lpcsdkft8v9ufsz3sa07/astro`. Because the admin can only burn, minting new ASTRO is impossible in practice.

- The Neutron Maker swaps 100% of its fees into ASTRO and deposits it here with `{"burn":{}}`.
- The Terra Maker sends 25% of Terra fees, bought as ASTRO, to the Terra satellite. The satellite IBC-transfers it to the burner (`transfer_astro`, callable by anyone), and a later `burn` call burns it.
- The Neutron Maker's seize config sends seized ASTRO here.
- The Neutron Treasury burn on 2026-10-08 burned 302,664,416.43 ASTRO through this contract.

ASTRO supply started at 1,000,000,000 and was 696,235,077.66 ASTRO on 2026-11-04. It only goes down. `total_burned` returns everything this contract has burned.

## ExecuteMsg

### `burn`

Burns the burner's whole balance of the denom, including funds attached to the call. If the call attaches any other denom, it fails. With nothing to burn, it does nothing.
