# Access Controls and Permissions

This page describes who controls the Astroport contracts under long-term support (LTS). It was checked against on-chain state on 2026-11-04.

## Control model

Token governance is retired: there are no ASTRO, xASTRO or vxASTRO votes and no Assembly proposals by token holders. Instead, the **"Astroport - Contributors" DAO DAO** on each chain controls the protocol. It doesn't own the contracts directly: it executes through the existing protocol owner contracts with `execute_from_multisig`.

- The Contributors DAO is the wasm admin of the protocol owner on its chain (and of itself).
- The protocol owner (Assembly or satellite) is the `owner` and the wasm admin of almost every other Astroport contract on its chain.
- The emergency multisigs stay as emergency owners. On Terra, the satellite's `emergency_owner` is the Terra emergency multisig. On both chains the emergency multisig owns the native coin registry and the builder unlock contract.
- The Development Fund DAOs pay for infrastructure and security. Their admin is the Contributors DAO. The Terra Development Fund receives 50% of Terra fees from the Maker.

## Owner powers

What each owner can change. Contracts not listed have no owner-only configuration.

### Factory

- Change `token_code_id`, `fee_address` (the Maker), `generator_address` (the Incentives contract), `whitelist_code_id` and `coin_registry_address`.
- Add or update a pool type's configuration: `code_id`, fees (`total_fee_bps`, `maker_fee_bps`), `is_disabled`, `is_generator_disabled` and `permissioned`.
- Deregister a pool (this also deactivates it in Incentives).
- Transfer ownership (two-step).

See [Factory](./factory).

### Pools

- Constant product pools: fee sharing, through `update_config`, by the factory owner.
- Stableswap pools: amplification changes and fee sharing, by the pool owner or the factory owner.
- PCL pools: parameter updates and fee sharing, by the pool owner or the factory owner.
- Exit-only pools: nothing. They only accept balanced withdrawals. See [Exit-only pools](./swap-pairs/exit-only-pools).

### Maker

- `update_config`: router, base asset, `max_spread`, collect cooldown, `collectors` and legs.
- `update_routes`: fee token routes to the base asset.
- `update_seize_config`: the seize receiver and the seizable assets.
- Transfer ownership (two-step).

`collect` and `seize` are permissionless today. See [Maker](./tokenomics/maker).

### yASTRO

- Reward denoms (at most 5 ever, never ASTRO), the unbonding period (at most 28 days, for new unbondings), the Incentives address and `min_lp_staked`.
- The owner can't move staked ASTRO or anyone's rewards.

See [yASTRO](./tokenomics/yastro).

### Incentives

- `update_config`: ASTRO token, vesting contract, generator controller, guardian, incentivization fee and token transfer gas limit.
- `update_fee_exemptions` (Terra 1.5.0): senders that don't pay the incentivization fee.
- `update_blocked_tokens_list` (owner or guardian), `remove_reward_from_pool`, `claim_orphaned_rewards`.
- `setup_pools` and `set_tokens_per_second` control ASTRO emissions, which are zero.

See [Incentives](./tokenomics/incentives).

### Vesting

- Register vesting accounts (paying the tokens in the same call) and withdraw from an account's active schedule.
- Transfer ownership (two-step).

### ASTRO burner and routers

No owner. The burner can only burn the ASTRO denom it administers; it has no message that mints or changes the denom admin. The routers have no configuration to change.

### Treasuries

The treasury admins can execute messages from the treasury and change the admin list. The only admin is the protocol owner (Assembly or satellite).

## Pausing and emergencies

There is no global pause in the Astroport contracts. The tools available to the protocol owner are:

- disable a pool type or deregister a pool in the factory;
- restrict `collect` on a Maker to an allowlist (`collectors`);
- migrate a contract through its wasm admin, as was done after the September 2026 incident: xASTRO staking was paused and retired (`astroport-staking` 2.3.3) and the six drained Neutron pools were migrated to `astroport-pair-exit-only`, which only allows balanced withdrawals.
