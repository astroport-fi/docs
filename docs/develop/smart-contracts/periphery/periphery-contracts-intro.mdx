# Periphery Contracts

Periphery contracts sit next to the core contracts and support them or the app.

- **Native coin registry** (`astroport-native-coin-registry` 1.1.0): stores the precision (decimals) of native coins. The factory and pools read it when a pool with a native coin is created. Its owner is the emergency multisig on each chain. Terra: [terra1zuf8fla02926nhpfvk09k2pg6qv9aayflp0qt4a0msppu2h4exqs6af275](https://terrasco.pe/mainnet/address/terra1zuf8fla02926nhpfvk09k2pg6qv9aayflp0qt4a0msppu2h4exqs6af275), Neutron: [neutron1jzzv6r5uckwd64n6qan3suzker0kct5w565f6529zjyumfcx96kqtcswn3](https://neutron.celat.one/neutron-1/contracts/neutron1jzzv6r5uckwd64n6qan3suzker0kct5w565f6529zjyumfcx96kqtcswn3). Source: [native_coin_registry](https://github.com/astroport-fi/astroport-core/tree/main/contracts/periphery/native_coin_registry).
- **Liquidity manager**: a helper for providing and withdrawing liquidity with a minimum LP amount, with `simulate_provide` and `simulate_withdraw` queries. Terra: [terra1pj43kem5evz2mhynahd20m7etktcy497rat3mfeh7y074heqfu9s48v7q4](https://terrasco.pe/mainnet/address/terra1pj43kem5evz2mhynahd20m7etktcy497rat3mfeh7y074heqfu9s48v7q4), Neutron: [neutron1wcnxmv6q2uq2nlme9f8ema6l37rkdtk3z7amvqdz39m88kdtzhfqllhv2m](https://neutron.celat.one/neutron-1/contracts/neutron1wcnxmv6q2uq2nlme9f8ema6l37rkdtk3z7amvqdz39m88kdtzhfqllhv2m). Current pools take `min_lp_to_receive` in `provide_liquidity` directly.
- **ASTRO converter** ([astro_converter](https://github.com/astroport-fi/astroport-core/tree/main/contracts/periphery/astro_converter)): converts the legacy ASTRO tokens to ASTRO, used by the converter pools. See [ASTRO Token](../token).
- **Tokenfactory tracker** ([tokenfactory_tracker](https://github.com/astroport-fi/astroport-core/tree/main/contracts/periphery/tokenfactory_tracker)): records balance history of a tokenfactory denom through a before-send hook.
- **Oracle**: a demo TWAP contract that isn't deployed. See [Oracle](../oracle).

All live addresses are on [Contract Addresses](../contract-addresses).
