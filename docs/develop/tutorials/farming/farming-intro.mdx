# Farming

Manage liquidity on Astroport from your own code:

- [Creating a Pair](creating-a-pair): create a new pool through the factory and find its address.
- [Providing/Withdrawing Liquidity](providing-withdrawing-liquidity): deposit assets for LP tokens, withdraw them again, and leave withdraw-only (exit-only) pools.
- [Staking/Unstaking LP Tokens](staking-unstaking-lp-tokens): stake LP tokens in the Incentives contract.
- [Claiming Rewards](claiming-rewards): claim incentive rewards and add incentive schedules to a pool.

Astroport no longer emits ASTRO to pools. Staked LP tokens earn the external incentive schedules that anyone can add to a pool. Contract addresses for Terra and Neutron are on the [Contract Addresses](/docs/develop/smart-contracts/contract-addresses) page.
