## Wallets

The wallets listed below can be used with Astroport on Terra and Neutron to store and transfer tokens and execute transactions.

No endorsement, recommendation, or guarantee is being made with respect to the use, functionality, or safety of these wallets. These wallets are listed for your convenience only. Do your own research and take precautions when using third-party wallets. There may be other wallets that also work with Astroport.

- **Keplr (Web):** https://www.keplr.app/
- **Terra Station (Web):** https://setup-station.terra.money/
- **Ledger (Hardware):** https://www.ledger.com/start/
