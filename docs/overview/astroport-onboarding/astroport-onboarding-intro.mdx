
# Astroport Onboarding

Astroport is the central space station of the DeFi solar system, where travelers throughout the galaxy meet to exchange assets in a neutral marketplace. The philosophy behind Astroport is simple:

> *Enabling decentralized, non-custodial liquidity and price discovery for any crypto asset.*

Astroport prioritizes flexibility, combining various specialized pool types and routing seamlessly across them. Please read the mission briefings on the following pages to make your trip to the station (https://app.astroport.fi) as comfortable as possible. 

Astroport runs on Terra and Neutron and is in [long-term support](/docs/learn/long-term-support): the contracts and the app stay live, security fixes come first, governance is retired and there are no ASTRO emissions.

Curious explorers can learn more about how to use Astroport on "Learn." Developers will find contract references and tutorials in "Develop."
