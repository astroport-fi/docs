# Security

Using Astroport doesn't come without risk. Before you decide to deposit your assets in the protocol or swap tokens, you should do your research and understand the risks involved. This section gives an overview of the main risks associated with the Astroport protocol.

Astroport is in [long-term support](/docs/learn/long-term-support): security fixes are the first priority for the contracts on Terra and Neutron.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Astroport no longer runs a bug bounty. If you find a vulnerability, contact the team on the [Astroport Discord](https://discord.astroport.fi). Don't share details of a vulnerability in public channels.

## Smart Contract Bugs

The Astroport contracts were audited by [Halborn](https://github.com/astroport-fi/astroport-audits/tree/main/halborn) and [Oak Security](https://github.com/astroport-fi/astroport-audits/tree/main/oak) (see [Audits](./audits)). However, security audits do not completely eliminate smart contract risk. We urge you not to put your life savings or money you can't afford to lose into Astroport.

## Chain Risk

Astroport runs on Terra and Neutron and depends on the security of those chains. In September 2026, a Neutron governance proposal took control of eight Astroport contracts on Neutron and drained ASTRO from xASTRO staking and six pools. The funds were returned after Neutron halted and restarted, but the incident shows that chain-level governance can affect contracts deployed on that chain. See [Long-Term Support](/docs/learn/long-term-support#september-2026-neutron-incident-and-recovery).

## Admin Control

The protocol contracts are owned by the Assembly on Neutron and the satellite on Terra, which are run by the Astroport Contributors DAOs, with emergency multisigs as emergency owners. The owners can change contract settings and migrate contracts. See [Who runs the protocol](/docs/learn/long-term-support#who-runs-the-protocol).

## Suboptimal Parameters

Pool creators and the protocol owners may set suboptimal parameters, for example:

- Amplification parameters for specific pools (e.g. pools where one asset accrues interest)
- PCL pool parameters that don't suit the pair's volatility (see [PCL Parameters](/docs/learn/astro-pools/passive-concentrated-liquidity-pools/pcl-parameters))
- Fee settings and fee routes in the Maker contracts
