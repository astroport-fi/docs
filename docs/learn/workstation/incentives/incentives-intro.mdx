Astroport's incentives are permissionless: anyone can incentivize any pool on Terra or Neutron, with up to five different reward tokens per pool. There are no ASTRO emissions anymore, so external incentives (and, on Terra, [yASTRO rewards for yASTRO pools](../../yastro#yastro-in-pools)) are the only incentives pools receive.

Here's how it works:

1. **Select a Pool:** Choose the liquidity pool you want to incentivize.
2. **Define Incentives:** Select the token(s) you want to incentivize the pool with.
3. **Set Duration:** Set the duration of the incentive, from 1 week to 25 weeks. Schedules run in Astroport weeks (Monday 00:00 UTC to Sunday 23:59 UTC): a new schedule starts right away and lasts until the next Monday plus the number of weeks you choose.
4. **Sign and Confirm:** Sign and confirm the transaction. Adding a reward token that the pool doesn't already have costs a 1,500 ASTRO incentivization fee, which goes to the Maker. Topping up a token the pool already has, or adding ASTRO as a reward, doesn't.

Continue below for an in-depth guide on deploying incentives.

### Step 1: Head over to [https://app.astroport.fi/pools](https://app.astroport.fi/pools) and select **Incentivize Pool**.

The **Incentivize Pool** button can be found on the top right of the **Pools** page. 

<img src={IncentivesUrl1} style={{ paddingBottom: 10 }} />

### Step 2: Select a pool to incentivize

Withdraw-only pools (the six Neutron pools drained in September 2026) can't usefully be incentivized: they don't accept deposits.

<img src={IncentivesUrl2} style={{ paddingBottom: 10 }} />

### Step 3: Define Incentives

First, we select the token we will be using to incentivize the pool. 

<img src={IncentivesUrl3} style={{ paddingBottom: 10 }} />

### Step 4: Set Incentives Amount

Next, we define the amount of incentives that are being deposited. 

<img src={IncentivesUrl4} style={{ paddingBottom: 10 }} />

### Step 5: Define Duration 

Lastly, we define the incentives schedule, or how many weeks your incentives will last. Incentives require a minimum of 1 week and a maximum of 25 weeks. 

Note that rewards per second must be at least 1 unit of the reward token. 

<img src={IncentivesUrl5} style={{ paddingBottom: 10 }} />

You can expand the duration tab to see incentives distributed per week for your defined schedule. 

<img src={IncentivesUrl5b} style={{ paddingBottom: 10 }} />

### Step 6: Sign and Confirm transaction

Once complete, your incentives schedule should begin! Rewards are paid to LPs who stake their LP tokens in the Incentives contract, pro rata to their stake.
