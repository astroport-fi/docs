# Workstation

Step-by-step tutorials for the Astroport app at [app.astroport.fi](https://app.astroport.fi), on Terra and Neutron:

- [**Starter Pack**](./starter-pack/starter-pack-intro): create and connect a wallet, buy ASTRO and bridge tokens
- [**Neutron Starter Pack**](./neutron-starter-pack/neutron-starter-pack-intro): get gas on Neutron and withdraw from the wstETH - WETH pool
- [**Traders Port**](./traders-port/traders-port-intro): swap tokens
- [**Farmers Fields**](./farmers-fields/farmers-fields-intro): create pools, provide and withdraw liquidity, stake LP tokens
- [**Deploying Incentives**](./incentives/incentives-intro): add incentives to a pool
- [**yASTRO**](../yastro): stake ASTRO on Terra and claim fee rewards
- [**Video Tutorials**](./video-tutorials)

Some Neutron pools are withdraw-only after the September 2026 incident: they allow withdrawals but no swaps or deposits. See [Long-Term Support](../long-term-support).
