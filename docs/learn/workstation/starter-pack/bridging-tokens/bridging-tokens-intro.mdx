# Bridging Tokens

Astroport runs on Terra (phoenix-1) and Neutron (neutron-1). Both are IBC-enabled Cosmos chains, so tokens move between them, and to and from other Cosmos chains, with IBC transfers.

## ASTRO on Terra and Neutron

ASTRO is native to Neutron (`factory/neutron1ffus553eet978k024lmssw0czsxwr97mggyv85lpcsdkft8v9ufsz3sa07/astro`). On Terra, ASTRO is the IBC version of the Neutron token (`ibc/8D8A7F7253615E5F76CB6252A1E1BD921D5EDB7BBAAF8913FB1C77FF125D9995`). To move ASTRO between the two chains, send it with an IBC transfer from a wallet or interface that supports Terra and Neutron.

The old ASTRO CW20 on Terra is legacy. Converting it to IBC ASTRO no longer works.

<Admonition type="info" title="yASTRO stays on Terra">
yASTRO is a CW20 token on Terra. Don't send it over IBC: the bridge escrow would hold it and earn its rewards instead of you. Unstake it first if you want to move your ASTRO.
</Admonition>

## From other chains

- **Cosmos chains:** use an IBC transfer, for example with Keplr or a cross-chain interface such as [IBC.fun](https://ibc.fun). Make sure you also have gas on the destination chain (LUNA on Terra, NTRN on Neutron).
- **EVM chains:** use a cross-chain router such as [Squid Router](https://www.squidrouter.com/) to bring assets to Terra or Neutron. See [Buying ASTRO](../buying-astro) for a step-by-step example.

No endorsement or guarantee is made about third-party bridges. Do your own research before using them.
