# Buying $ASTRO

<img src={BuyingAstroUrl} style={{ paddingBottom: 10 }} />

ASTRO can be swapped for on Astroport on Neutron and Terra at [app.astroport.fi](https://app.astroport.fi). Check the pools page in the app for the current ASTRO markets and their liquidity.

- **Neutron:** ASTRO is native (`factory/neutron1ffus553eet978k024lmssw0czsxwr97mggyv85lpcsdkft8v9ufsz3sa07/astro`).
- **Terra:** ASTRO is the IBC version of the Neutron token (`ibc/8D8A7F7253615E5F76CB6252A1E1BD921D5EDB7BBAAF8913FB1C77FF125D9995`). This is the ASTRO you can stake as [yASTRO](../../yastro).

There are two primary ways to obtain ASTRO:

1. Centralized approach: Use a CEX to buy the native token of Neutron (NTRN) or Terra (LUNA). Then, withdraw it to a wallet on that chain and swap it for $ASTRO at app.astroport.fi.
2. Decentralized approach: Bridge tokens to Neutron or Terra with a cross-chain router such as Squid Router or IBC.fun. Then, swap those bridged tokens for $ASTRO at app.astroport.fi.

This tutorial will guide you through swapping for $ASTRO starting from either a centralized exchange or an EVM chain. 

## Starting from a CEX

You can transfer either a volatile or stable token to Neutron or Terra and swap it for $ASTRO on-chain. 

### Bridging a Volatile Pair Asset

One of the simplest methods for buying $ASTRO starting from a CEX is to withdraw a gas token and then swap that token for $ASTRO on-chain. E.g. you can purchase $NTRN or $LUNA on a centralized exchange, withdraw it to Neutron or Terra and swap it for $ASTRO. However, you may be exposed to price changes between the time you buy/transfer the gas token and when you swap that token for $ASTRO. 

<Admonition type="info" title="NOTE">
These tokens also serve as gas tokens for their respective chains, so make sure to keep some remaining in your wallet for additional transactions. 
</Admonition>

This tutorial will walk you through transferring $NTRN from Binance to the Neutron chain and swapping $NTRN for $ASTRO. You can follow similar steps for $LUNA on Terra.

### Bridging a Stablecoin

Purchase $USDC and withdraw it to an EVM chain (e.g. Arbitrum, Optimism, Base). Bridge your USDC to Neutron or Terra using [Squid Router](https://www.squidrouter.com/). Get some $NTRN or $LUNA for gas, then swap your USDC for $ASTRO on Astroport.

## Starting from an EVM Chain

If you want to bridge from an EVM chain, the best route is to transfer stablecoins to swap for $ASTRO. You will also need some gas on the destination chain (NTRN on Neutron, LUNA on Terra). If you can't get gas from a centralized exchange, a cross-chain router such as Squid Router can deliver a small amount of gas on-chain. Then bridge your USDC to Neutron or Terra with [Squid Router](https://www.squidrouter.com/) and swap it for $ASTRO on Astroport.

For a step-by-step example of bridging stables from EVM to Neutron using Squid Router and then swapping for $ASTRO, refer to the [section above](#bridging-a-stablecoin).
