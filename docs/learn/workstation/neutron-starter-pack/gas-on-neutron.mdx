# Gas on Neutron

Every transaction on Neutron needs a small amount of gas, paid in NTRN, Neutron's native token.

The simplest way to get gas is to buy NTRN on a centralized exchange that lists it and withdraw it directly to your Neutron address. Check the exchange's withdrawal fee and minimum before you withdraw.

If you already hold tokens on another chain, you can bring them to Neutron instead:

- **From a Cosmos chain:** send tokens to Neutron with an IBC transfer, using your wallet or a cross-chain interface such as [IBC.fun](https://ibc.fun).
- **From an EVM chain:** use a cross-chain router such as [Squid Router](https://www.squidrouter.com/), which can also deliver a small amount of gas on the destination chain.

Once you have NTRN in your Neutron wallet, you can swap it on Astroport at [app.astroport.fi](https://app.astroport.fi). Keep some NTRN for future transactions. See [Buying ASTRO](../starter-pack/buying-astro) for a full example.

No endorsement or guarantee is made about third-party exchanges, bridges or routers. Do your own research before using them.
