# Learn

Get ready to explore Astroport. Whether you're just starting with DeFi or already know your way around AMMs, these pages explain how Astroport works and how to use it.

Astroport is in [long-term support](./long-term-support): the contracts on Terra and Neutron and the app at [app.astroport.fi](https://app.astroport.fi) stay live, governance is retired and there are no ASTRO emissions.

- [**Long-Term Support**](./long-term-support): how Astroport runs today, the September 2026 incident and recovery, and where fees go
- [**yASTRO**](./yastro): stake ASTRO on Terra and earn a share of Terra fees in USDC.inj
- [**Astro Pools**](./astro-pools/astro-pools-intro): the liquidity pool types of Astroport
- [**Astro Incentives**](./astro-incentives): how LP rewards work
- [**Astro Tokenomics**](./astro-tokenomics/astro-tokenomics-intro): ASTRO supply, utility and fees
- [**Workstation**](./workstation/workstation-intro): step-by-step tutorials for the Astroport app
