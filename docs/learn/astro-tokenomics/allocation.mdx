# Supply and Allocation

## Supply

| | ASTRO |
|---|---|
| Starting supply | 1,000,000,000 |
| Burned by the Neutron Treasury burn (2026-10-08) | 302,664,416.43 |
| Supply on 2026-11-04 | 696,235,077.66 |

The ASTRO supply only goes down:

- **Minting has ended.** The admin of the ASTRO denom on Neutron is the ASTRO burner contract ([neutron1wl7akesary9ac3h5k3mjw3lj5e93pvpwlc8xjh05aug7zgl084gq8xhjxp](https://neutron.celat.one/neutron-1/contracts/neutron1wl7akesary9ac3h5k3mjw3lj5e93pvpwlc8xjh05aug7zgl084gq8xhjxp)), which can only burn. No new ASTRO can be created.
- **Fees burn ASTRO.** All of Neutron's protocol fees and 25% of Terra's are used to buy ASTRO, which the burner burns. See [Fees](./fees).
- **No emissions.** ASTRO emissions to pools have ended.

The Neutron Treasury ([neutron19sq60cxtsjcx7vw25c63wyt27fxevuh6l7vxcn04u0t0rueyfpvq4mc75l](https://neutron.celat.one/neutron-1/contracts/neutron19sq60cxtsjcx7vw25c63wyt27fxevuh6l7vxcn04u0t0rueyfpvq4mc75l)) keeps about 60M ASTRO, reserved for future protocol-owned liquidity.

## Historical allocation

<Admonition type="info" title="Historical">
This section describes the original ASTRO allocation plan from Astroport's launch on Terra in 2022. It no longer applies: ASTRO emissions to LPs have ended, the Assembly's governance role is retired, and the current supply is shown above.
</Admonition>

At launch, ASTRO was allocated to an airdrop for the community (12%), liquidity provider emissions (45%), the Astroport Assembly treasury (16%) and the Astroport builders, with a 3-year lockup (27%). Astroport did not have an ICO.
