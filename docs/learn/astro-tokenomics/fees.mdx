# Fees

## Overview

Traders pay a fee on every swap, in the token they sell. Part of the fee stays in the pool for its liquidity providers (LPs) and part goes to the Maker contract on that chain, which turns it into ASTRO burns, yASTRO rewards and development funding. The fee settings are part of each pool's configuration.

## Pool fees

The defaults below are the factory settings for new pools on Terra and Neutron. Individual pools can have their own fee parameters.

### Constant product pools

The fee is 0.3%:

- 0.2% (2/3 of the fee) goes to the pool's LPs. It stays in the pool, which increases the number of tokens LPs can redeem when they withdraw.
- 0.1% (1/3 of the fee) goes to the Maker.

### Passive concentrated liquidity (PCL) pools

The fee is variable and depends on the pool's parameters (for example 0.26% to 0.45%, or lower for liquid staking pairs):

- 50% of the fee goes to the pool's LPs, as above.
- 50% of the fee goes to the Maker.

### Stableswap pools

The fee is 0.05%:

- 0.025% (1/2 of the fee) goes to the pool's LPs.
- 0.025% (1/2 of the fee) goes to the Maker.

### Withdraw-only pools

The six Neutron pools made withdraw-only after the September 2026 incident don't accept swaps, so they don't earn or pay fees.

## Where the Maker's share goes

Each Maker collects the fees its pools sent it, converts them along a set of routes and distributes them. Collection is permissionless and runs weekly (Sunday evening UTC). Neither Maker ever sells ASTRO.

### Neutron

The Neutron Maker converts fees into NTRN, then buys ASTRO with 100% of it and deposits it into the ASTRO burner, which burns it. Neutron fees are burn only.

### Terra

The Terra Maker converts fees into USDC.inj, then splits them:

| Share | Destination |
|---|---|
| 25% | Bought into ASTRO and sent over IBC to the ASTRO burner on Neutron, which burns it |
| 25% | Deposited into [yASTRO](../yastro) and streamed to yASTRO stakers over the following week |
| 50% | Sent to the Terra Astroport Development Fund (infrastructure and security) |

See [Long-Term Support](../long-term-support#where-fees-go) for more context, and the [Maker](/docs/develop/smart-contracts/tokenomics/maker) and [ASTRO burner](/docs/develop/smart-contracts/tokenomics/astro-burner) contract references for details.
