# Utility

## Overview

ASTRO's main utility today is staking it as **yASTRO** on Terra to earn a share of protocol fees. ASTRO no longer has a governance role.

## yASTRO: fee share

- Stake ASTRO on Terra and receive yASTRO 1:1 at [app.astroport.fi/yastro](https://app.astroport.fi/yastro).
- Each week 25% of the fees collected by the Terra Maker are deposited into yASTRO in USDC.inj and streamed per second over the following week to yASTRO holders, pro rata to their balance.
- Claim rewards at any time from the Rewards Center. Unstaking takes 7 days, and unbonding ASTRO earns nothing.
- yASTRO is transferable. Pools that hold yASTRO earn rewards too, and these can be forwarded to the pool's staked LPs.

Read the full guide in [yASTRO](../yastro).

## Other uses

- **Trading and liquidity.** ASTRO trades in Astroport pools on Neutron and Terra and can be provided as liquidity like any other token.
- **Incentivization fee.** Adding a new reward token to a pool's incentives costs a 1,500 ASTRO fee, paid to the Maker. See [Deploying Incentives](../workstation/incentives/incentives-intro).

## What ASTRO no longer does

- **Governance is retired.** There are no ASTRO, xASTRO or vxASTRO votes, no token holder proposals and no emissions voting. The protocol is run by the Astroport Contributors DAOs (see [Long-Term Support](../long-term-support#who-runs-the-protocol)).
- **xASTRO is retired.** xASTRO used to be the staking token that accrued ASTRO from fees and carried voting power. After the September 2026 Neutron incident it was frozen and retired; it has no value and can't be redeemed. Pre-incident holders were paid in ASTRO at 1.3402 ASTRO per xASTRO.
- **vxASTRO is winding down.** Existing vxASTRO positions only unwind through their normal unlock. They no longer vote or direct emissions.
