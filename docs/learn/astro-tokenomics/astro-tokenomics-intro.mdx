# Astro Tokenomics

## Overview

ASTRO is Astroport's token. It is native to Neutron and is used on Terra through IBC.

| Chain | ASTRO denom |
|---|---|
| Neutron | `factory/neutron1ffus553eet978k024lmssw0czsxwr97mggyv85lpcsdkft8v9ufsz3sa07/astro` |
| Terra | `ibc/8D8A7F7253615E5F76CB6252A1E1BD921D5EDB7BBAAF8913FB1C77FF125D9995` |

The old ASTRO CW20 on Terra (`terra1nsuqsk6kh58ulczatwev87ttq2z6r3pusulg9r24mfj2fvtzd4uq3exn26`) is legacy. Converting it to IBC ASTRO no longer works.

With Astroport in [long-term support](../long-term-support), ASTRO works like this:

- **Supply only goes down.** The supply started at 1,000,000,000 ASTRO and was 696,235,077.66 ASTRO on 2026-11-04. New ASTRO can't be minted, and protocol fees keep buying and burning ASTRO. See [Supply and Allocation](./allocation).
- **No emissions.** ASTRO is no longer emitted to pools.
- **No governance.** ASTRO, xASTRO and vxASTRO no longer vote on anything.
- **Fee share through yASTRO.** On Terra, ASTRO can be staked as [yASTRO](../yastro) to earn a quarter of Terra's protocol fees in USDC.inj. See [Utility](./utility).
- **Fees burn ASTRO.** All of Neutron's protocol fees and a quarter of Terra's buy ASTRO, which is burned. See [Fees](./fees).
